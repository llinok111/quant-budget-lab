what this proves

Each primitive owns its grid, so max error is minimized for the whole byte budget instead of being fixed by the largest part. The claim is a bound, not a hope: sampled surface error (point→original-triangle, a separate oracle) must stay under it. Node replays the identical plan: node bench/verify.mjs.
